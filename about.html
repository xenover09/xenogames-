What this site is

Xeno Games collects the most interesting game mashups, fan ports and crossovers in one place — and makes each one install with a single click.

Great mods are scattered across forums, file hosts and dead threads. Most players never try them because installing one means following a 30-step guide, matching exact versions, and hoping nothing breaks. We find the best ones, verify the files, and turn each into a Play button.

How it works

  • Browse the library and pick a mashup.
  • Press Play — the launcher finds your games, sets up what's needed, and installs the files.
  • Every original file is backed up; uninstalling restores everything.

Community

Players share what they built, help each other with installs, and vote on the next crossover in our Discord.
